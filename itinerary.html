Getting here

From Hanoi to the mountains

Before your tour
  1. Hanoi pickupMeeting point agreed
  2. Coach journeyIncluded · classic tours
  3. Ha Giang CityMeet & start your loop

Hanoi pickup and coach travel to Ha Giang City are included in the published 3-day and 4-day prices. Confirm your meeting point, departure time and coach type before travel. Transfer time is separate from the loop days. Private and onward-transfer arrangements follow your quote.

  1. 1Day 1Ha Giang to Du Gia+
    • Ha Giang
    • Bac Sum Pass
    • Quan Ba Twin Mountains
    • Pine-hill stop
    • Du Gia

    Leave Ha Giang and follow the mountain roads towards Quan Ba. Pause at Bac Sum and the Twin Mountains viewpoints, then take a pine-hill break before continuing to Du Gia. The local team confirms the pine viewpoint and connecting road to suit the day's conditions and timing.

    Proposed overnight: Du Gia.

  2. 2Day 2Waterfall, river & high mountain views+
    • Du Gia Waterfall
    • Nho Que River
    • Ma Pi Leng Pass
    • Skywalk
    • Dong Van

    Start with Du Gia Waterfall, then continue towards the Nho Que River and Ma Pi Leng area. River access and pass viewpoints are sequenced locally to allow time for the descent and return. A boat trip is an option to confirm in the quote, not an automatic inclusion. Add a Skywalk viewpoint walk only when local access, weather and the group's ability allow; finish in Dong Van.

    Proposed overnight: Dong Van. This is the most activity-heavy day.

  3. 3Day 3Village craft & the road back+
    • Dong Van
    • Tham Ma Pass
    • Yen Minh
    • Weaving village
    • Waterfall stop
    • Ha Giang

    Return via Tham Ma Pass and Yen Minh. Lung Tam is the proposed weaving-village visit in the Quan Ba area, offering a chance to learn about Hmong hemp textiles and handweaving when the workshop is open. A final waterfall stop is subject to a suitable location and time on the return route, agreed before booking. Finish in Ha Giang and arrange onward-transfer timing separately.

    Finish: Ha Giang. No overnight stay included in this itinerary after the return.

Full 3-day itinerary ↗
  1. 1Day 1Ha Giang, Quan Ba & Duong Thuong+
    • Ha Giang
    • Bac Sum Pass
    • Quan Ba Twin Mountains
    • Duong Thuong
    • Du Gia

    Begin in Ha Giang and climb towards Bac Sum Pass and the Quan Ba Twin Mountains. A pine-hill stop offers a change of pace before the valley roads through Duong Thuong and the onward journey to Du Gia. The local team confirms the exact pine viewpoint and road connection before departure.

    Proposed overnight: Du Gia.

  2. 2Day 2Du Gia to the Nho Que & Ma Pi Leng area+
    • Du Gia Waterfall
    • Nho Que River
    • Ma Pi Leng Pass
    • Skywalk
    • Dong Van

    Visit Du Gia Waterfall, then travel towards the dramatic river-and-pass scenery around Ma Pi Leng. The Nho Que visit, optional boat arrangements and viewpoint stops are timed together to avoid a rushed river descent. A Skywalk visit is conditional on access, conditions and suitable ability. Continue to Dong Van for the proposed second night.

    Proposed overnight: Dong Van. Allow flexibility on this activity-heavy day.

  3. 3Day 3Lung Cu & the northern frontier+
    • Dong Van
    • Lung Cu Flag Tower
    • Vietnam-side border viewpoint
    • Tham Ma Pass
    • Yen Minh

    Head north towards Lung Cu Flag Tower for the frontier chapter of the journey. A locally permitted border viewpoint on the Vietnam side can be considered with the guide; this is not a crossing into China. Return towards Tham Ma Pass and Yen Minh, with the exact frontier stop agreed locally before booking.

    Proposed overnight: Yen Minh.

  4. 4Day 4Weaving traditions & return to Ha Giang+
    • Yen Minh
    • Weaving village
    • Waterfall stop
    • Ha Giang

    Continue towards Quan Ba for the proposed Lung Tam weaving-village visit. Learn about Hmong hemp cloth and the patient work behind local textiles; workshop access is confirmed in advance. Include the requested final waterfall only once the team has confirmed a suitable location and time on the return route. Finish in Ha Giang, with any onward transfer arranged separately.

    Finish: Ha Giang. No border crossing is part of this tour.
