Before you go

Good questions. Clear answers.

Should I choose the 3-day or 4-day Ha Giang Loop?

Both routes feature Du Gia, Nho Que River and Ma Pi Leng. The 3-day version returns on day three; the 4-day version adds a Lung Cu and northern-frontier day, plus a proposed third night in Yen Minh.

Is the Nho Que boat trip included?

Not automatically. Boat availability, tickets, transfer arrangements and inclusion in the price must be confirmed in your written quote.

Does the route cross the Chinese border?

No. The 4-day itinerary may include a locally permitted viewpoint on the Vietnam side. The exact stop is confirmed with the local team.

Which weaving village and return-day waterfall will we visit?

Lung Tam is the proposed weaving-village stop, subject to workshop access and route timing. The return-day waterfall location and feasibility must be confirmed with the team before booking.

What are the prices and how do I get from Hanoi to Ha Giang?

The prices are USD 200–226 per person for 3 days / 2 nights and USD 250–270 per person for 4 days / 3 nights, including Hanoi pickup and coach travel to Ha Giang City. Your exact price, coach type and timing are confirmed before travel. Other services and return or onward transport are itemised in writing. Private tours receive a tailored quote.

How does the deposit work?

First request your dates, group size and route. Confirm the written price, services and cancellation terms, then pay only the agreed deposit to a verified official recipient. The deposit amount and payment method have not yet been published. This demonstration website does not send enquiries, take deposits or confirm bookings.

Can weather change the itinerary?

Yes. Road conditions, river access, waterfall conditions and viewpoint access can change the plan. Skywalk visits are conditional; do not enter closed trails or stop for photos on an active roadway.
