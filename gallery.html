Verified Experience · 3-Day & 4-Day Group LoopDu Gia Forest Trail & Emerald Waterfall

“Rain ponchos on the morning trail, swimming at Du Gia Waterfall by afternoon”

When a passing mountain shower rolled through the forest trail toward Du Gia, our Easy Riders immediately handed out colorful ponchos and kept the whole group of eight laughing together. By the time we reached Du Gia Waterfall, the sun was out and we spent the afternoon swimming in the cool emerald pool right below the cascade.

Verified Experience · Ma Pi Leng & SkywalkMa Pi Leng Cliff Ledge & Panorama Stop

“Standing on the Ma Pi Leng cliff rock & earning that #9 Finisher jersey”

Our local driver knew the exact morning window when the clouds lifted off Ma Pi Leng so we could walk out to the overhanging cliff rock safely and without a crowd. Celebrating afterward at the wooden viewpoint railing in the #9 Finisher “Một Hai Ba, Dzô!” jersey with an iced coffee overlooking the karst peaks felt surreal.

Verified Experience · Cultural StopNho Que 0Km Milestone · Hmong Brocade

“Trying on traditional hand-embroidered Hmong attire at Nho Que 0Km”

Before descending into the gorge, our guide suggested a relaxed cultural stop at the Nho Que 0Km milestone. Dressing in traditional red-and-black Hmong brocade with silver necklaces and holding the bamboo khèn flute while the mist drifted across the peaks behind us became our favorite portraits of the entire trip.

Verified Experience · Canyon Boat TripNho Que River & Tu San Canyon

“Sitting on the bow as our boat glided between 800-meter limestone walls”

Looking down at the Nho Que River from the top of the pass is breathtaking, but boarding the wooden boat down on the water gives you a completely different perspective. Sitting right on the bow as we glided across calm jade-green water into the towering vertical cliffs of Tu San Canyon was peaceful and awe-inspiring.

Verified Experience · 4-Day Frontier RouteLung Cu Flag Tower · Northernmost Point

“Parking our line of motorbikes beneath Lung Cu Flag Tower on Day 3”

We debated between the 3-day and 4-day routes, and choosing the 4-day loop for the northern frontier day was the best decision we made. Parking our row of tour motorbikes at the base of Lung Cu Flag Tower and standing with open arms beneath the giant red flag in the cool mountain mist felt like a true expedition.

“Playful stops on the Honda Winner 150 & camping chairs above a sea of clouds”
