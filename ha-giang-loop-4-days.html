Ha Giang Loop · 4 Days

Follow the longer loop with an extra day for Lung Cu and the northern frontier. Travel from Ha Giang to Du Gia, Dong Van and Yen Minh before returning to your starting point. With a proposed third night in Yen Minh, the frontier sights have their own day and village craft rounds off the journey home.

Day by day

Your proposed journey

Proposed itinerary: the exact stops and overnight arrangements are agreed before booking. Weather, road conditions and local access may change the order or availability of activities.

Day 1

Ha Giang, Quan Ba & Duong Thuong

+

Ha Giang → Bac Sum Pass → Quan Ba Twin Mountains → pine-hill stop / Duong Thuong → Du Gia

Begin in Ha Giang and climb towards Bac Sum Pass and the Quan Ba Twin Mountains. A pine-hill stop offers a change of pace before the valley roads through Duong Thuong and the onward journey to Du Gia. The local team confirms the exact pine viewpoint and road connection before departure.

Proposed overnight: Du Gia.

Day 2

Du Gia to the Nho Que & Ma Pi Leng area

+

Du Gia Waterfall → Nho Que River / Ma Pi Leng → Skywalk → Dong Van

Visit Du Gia Waterfall, then travel towards the dramatic river-and-pass scenery around Ma Pi Leng. The Nho Que visit, optional boat arrangements and viewpoint stops are timed together to avoid a rushed river descent. A Skywalk visit is conditional on access, conditions and suitable ability. Continue to Dong Van for the proposed second night.

Proposed overnight: Dong Van. Allow flexibility on this activity-heavy day.

Day 3

Lung Cu & the northern frontier

+

Dong Van → Lung Cu Flag Tower → Vietnam-side border viewpoint → Tham Ma Pass → Yen Minh

Head north towards Lung Cu Flag Tower for the frontier chapter of the journey. A locally permitted border viewpoint on the Vietnam side can be considered with the guide; this is not a crossing into China. Return towards Tham Ma Pass and Yen Minh, with the exact frontier stop agreed locally before booking.

Proposed overnight: Yen Minh.

Day 4

Weaving traditions & return to Ha Giang

+

Yen Minh → weaving village → waterfall stop → Ha Giang

Continue towards Quan Ba for the proposed Lung Tam weaving-village visit. Learn about Hmong hemp cloth and the patient work behind local textiles; workshop access is confirmed in advance. Include the requested final waterfall only once the team has confirmed a suitable location and time on the return route. Finish in Ha Giang, with any onward transfer arranged separately.

Finish: Ha Giang. No border crossing is part of this tour.

Before you go

Good questions. Clear answers.

Should I choose the 3-day or 4-day Ha Giang Loop?

Both routes feature Du Gia, Nho Que River and Ma Pi Leng. The 3-day version returns on day three; the 4-day version adds a Lung Cu and northern-frontier day, plus a proposed third night in Yen Minh.

Is the Nho Que boat trip included?

Not automatically. Boat availability, tickets, transfer arrangements and inclusion in the price must be confirmed in your written quote.

Does the route cross the Chinese border?

No. The 4-day itinerary may include a locally permitted viewpoint on the Vietnam side. The exact stop is confirmed with the local team.

Which weaving village and return-day waterfall will we visit?

Lung Tam is the proposed weaving-village stop, subject to workshop access and route timing. The return-day waterfall location and feasibility must be confirmed with the team before booking.

How do I request a price and arrange transport?

Choose your itinerary and prepare your dates, group size and preferred travel style. Transport, rooms, meals and fees require written confirmation. Contact details are currently demo placeholders: the form prepares a draft to copy, but does not send it, take payment or confirm a reservation.

Can weather change the itinerary?

Yes. Road conditions, river access, waterfall conditions and viewpoint access can change the plan. Skywalk visits are conditional; do not enter closed trails or stop for photos on an active roadway.
